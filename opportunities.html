Opportunities

Experiences, access, and support built into the CIA path

Consumer Insights & Analytics is designed to give students more than coursework. The program also emphasizes client projects, competitions, internships, scholarships, mentoring, and a strong cohort and alumni network.

Client Projects Competitions Internships Scholarships Mentors Alumni Network
Client projects

Applied work with real business context

Real-world analysis

Students are trained to transform consumer and transaction data into business recommendations, then present those findings as actionable solutions to real-world problems.

Professional presentation

The major emphasizes presenting insights to executives, identifying trends, and communicating current recommendations with confidence.

Competitions

Regional and national competition experience

Analytics Club involvement

Active members of the Analytics Club participate in regional undergraduate competitions, giving students chances to solve real problems under pressure and work as a team.

Winning culture

Student success stories like Team Data Doodlers show that CIA students are building the confidence and preparation needed to compete successfully beyond campus.

Internships

Internships with strong support

In-house internship program

According to the official UMD academics page, LSBE has a dedicated internship director who helps oversee more than 200 internships annually and works with both students and employers.

Top internship destinations

Students have secured internships at organizations such as 3M, Google, Hormel, Fastenal, Land O’Lakes, Minnesota Vikings, Target, UnitedHealth Group, Sherwin-Williams, Taylor Corporation, and SPS Commerce.

Scholarships

Funding and professional support

LSBE scholarships

The official UMD academics page notes that LSBE awards more than 150 scholarships each year to students across business majors and academic paths.

Certification support

Students also receive certification exam preparation and paid exam fees for Tableau and Power BI, adding another practical advantage for internships and early career opportunities.

Mentors

Guidance from experienced leaders

Program leadership

The official UMD academics page highlights that the program director brings more than 30 years of experience in retail, broadcast television, and magazine publishing, and supports teaching, internships, networking, and the advisory council.

Professional development

Students also benefit from professional development opportunities such as the Passport Program, LSBE Company Days, guest speakers, and connections through the CIA Club and broader business community.

Alumni network

A cohort that continues beyond graduation

Selected cohort model

Your recruiting materials describe the program as a selected group of top students and alumni, reinforcing the idea that CIA is both an academic path and a close professional network.

Lifelong connections

Student testimonials from the recruiting packet emphasize friendships, shared problem-solving, and lasting connections that continue far beyond graduation.

Recruiting focus The CIA experience is built around three things students remember most: meaningful experiences, access to real opportunities, and a cohort community that feels personal and lasting.

Source material used on this page came from your recruiting PDF and the official UMD academics page for the Consumer Insights & Analytics B.B.A.
