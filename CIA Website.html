Consumer insights and analytics

Create actionable insights from customer data

Consumer Insights & Analytics focuses on turning consumer behavior and transaction data into business recommendations. The program connects customer understanding, data analysis, and strategic decision-making.

The program is designed for students who want to study how people interact with products, services, and the marketplace, then use data to support stronger strategic decisions.

Program overview

What students can expect

Students build a foundation in business, analytics, and communication while working with tools and datasets used in real organizations.

Program overview

Students learn to transform consumer data related to buying behaviors into insights and actionable recommendations for organizations.

Hands-on environment

Students gain access to lab resources, specialized software, industry datasets, and analytics competition opportunities.

Business communication

Homepage copy also emphasizes presenting insights as solutions to business problems rather than reporting data in isolation.

CIA club

A student community built around experience, access, and cohorts

The CIA Club helps students build connections, gain professional exposure, and grow alongside peers with shared interests in insights and analytics.

Experiences

Club programming can include guest speakers, competitions, projects, and opportunities that connect classroom learning to real industry experience.

Access

Students gain access to industry perspectives, networking opportunities, and guidance that helps them explore careers and strengthen professional readiness.

Cohorts

The club supports a strong cohort experience by helping students build relationships, collaborate, and grow as a close learning community.
