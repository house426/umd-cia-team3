Consumer insights and analytics

Create actionable insights from customer data

Do you aspire to help organizations make better strategic decisions based on data rather than opinion? Does the prospect of analyzing data to reveal insights excite you? Would you like to study why and how consumers interact with the marketplace, products, and services?

If so, the Consumer Insights and Analytics major may be a great fit for you. Students learn to transform consumer data related to buying behaviors into insights and actionable recommendations for organizations, then present those insights as solutions to real business problems.

Program overview

What students can expect

The major blends customer understanding, analytics, communication, and hands-on learning so students can build practical experience before graduation.

Analytics with purpose

Students study how consumers interact with products, services, and the marketplace, then use that data to support stronger decisions.

Hands-on learning

Students have 24/7 access to a state-of-the-art analytics lab with sophisticated software tools and industry datasets.

Business communication

Students learn to present their insights as solutions to business problems rather than simply reporting numbers in isolation.

Student spotlight

Team Data Doodlers took first place

CIA students are not only learning analytics in the classroom. They are also competing and succeeding in high-level events beyond campus.

The winning team

Team Data Doodlers was made up of LSBE CIA students Jordan Carlson, Megan Gehling, Ella Green, Reilly Sullivan, Laura Tsai, and Natalya Wilkes.

The team recently competed in the undergraduate division of the Analytics for Good Hackathon at the University of Minnesota Carlson School of Management.

The result

They won first place and were recognized as champions of the event.

This achievement reflects the kind of preparation, teamwork, and applied problem-solving that the CIA program and club are designed to support.

Why LSBE loves this Winning teams, strong cohorts, and visible student success show what makes Consumer Insights & Analytics more than a major. It is a community that prepares students to compete and lead.
